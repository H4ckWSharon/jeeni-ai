Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026  ·  Domain: jeeni-ai.com

JEENI AI ("Jeeni", "we", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of students, educators, and learners who use our website (jeeni-ai.com), web app (jeeni-ai.com/app/), and Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account via email/password or Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication, we store your display name, verified email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Learning Profile & Preferences: Optional curriculum settings you provide (such as class/grade, subjects, and learning goals) so Jeeni can tailor explanations.
  • Conversations & Uploaded Study Materials: Prompts, text questions, images, or study files you send in standard chats so you can revisit your history across sessions.

2. Incognito / Private Study Mode

When you switch to Incognito (Temporary Chat) mode inside Jeeni AI, conversations are kept ephemeral in your session and are not saved to your persistent chat history.

3. How We Use Information

  • Deliver accurate AI tutoring, interactive visualizations, and web-grounded explanations.
  • Verify email ownership to prevent unauthorized or automated spam accounts.
  • Sync your subject folders, favorites, and learning memory across web and mobile sessions.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal information to third-party advertisers. All data transmitted between your device, Vercel edge servers, Firebase Authentication, and our backend API uses encrypted protocols and strict authentication checks.

5. Managing or Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual chat sessions directly from your sidebar history or delete/manage your profile preferences inside Profile & Settings in the Jeeni app.
